# v2 results: weight tuning and sensitivity (k=5, 7 listeners)

Generated by `python -m experiments.run_v2`. Metric: mean NDCG@5 across listeners.

## 1. Does tuning the weights help?

Random search over 800 weight settings (each of genre, mood, energy and acoustic scaled between 0.25x and 4x). Ties go to the setting closest to the current weights.

| | Current weights | Best found |
|---|---|---|
| genre | 1.25 | 0.51 |
| mood | 1.50 | 0.71 |
| energy | 2.00 | 0.95 |
| acoustic | 0.60 | 0.58 |
| **NDCG@5 on all 7 listeners (optimistic)** | 0.586 | 0.616 |

Only 1 of 800 settings reaches that best score: a narrow peak, which is a warning sign for overfitting.
