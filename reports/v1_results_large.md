# v1 results (large catalog): baselines and ablation (k=5, 1985 songs, 60 listeners in 5 groups)

Generated by `python -m experiments.run_v1 --dataset large`. **Genre-taste protocol** (synthetic): each archetype likes every song in its genres; a listener is 3 random seed songs from them; relevant = the archetype's other songs. Because taste is *defined by genre*, any method that returns the seeds' genre is favoured, so read these numbers as a sanity check, not as a ranking of methods. Mood here is derived from energy and valence. The 95% interval resamples whole archetypes.
