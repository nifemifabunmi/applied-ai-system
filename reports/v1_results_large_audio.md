# v1 results (large_audio catalog): baselines and ablation (k=5, 1985 songs, 60 listeners in 10 groups)

Generated by `python -m experiments.run_v1 --dataset large_audio`. **Audio-taste protocol** (synthetic): same seeds, but a listener's taste is the 100 songs closest to the seeds in a richer audio space (speechiness, instrumentalness, liveness and loudness, which the recommender never sees, plus its own features). Taste is not defined by genre, so similarity-based methods are favoured instead. Mood is derived from energy and valence. The 95% interval resamples whole archetypes.
